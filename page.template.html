Trade goods

Every trade commodity you are carrying, grouped by the category the game shows in its description. Each category is produced by one economy and wanted by another, so the economy to look for on the galaxy map is named against each group. The game gives every economy four different names — hover any economy to see all of them.

Watch the word "Construction". As an economy it means Manufacturing — buy Minerals there. As a trade-good category it is something else entirely, and those sell in Scientific. Same word, two unrelated things.

The two trade loops

Buy where a category is produced, sell in the next system along. Both loops close, so you can run either indefinitely.
